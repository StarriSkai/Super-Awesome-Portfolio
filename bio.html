About me

I was born in 2001... I am an Aquarius... I like long walks on the beach... HA! GOT YA! Now for the real stuff-

I have an Associates in Studio Arts from San Joaquin Delta College, and have been enrolled in art classes since I was 4 years old. Now I am more digitally focused, utilising Clip Studio Paint alongside Adobe software for my artwork. Currently, my artistic skills are used to design characters, create album art, posters, stickers, and clothing. Once in a blue moon I have the chance to create for myself, now experimenting with mixed media to find the perfect marriage of artistic skills learned throughout my life.
